How to get cited in Perplexity answers

Perplexity searches the web for every question and quotes a handful of sources. This guide covers how it picks them, and the work that makes your pages one of them.

Buyers now ask Perplexity the questions they used to type into Google: which tool fits a team like ours, how two products compare, what a fair price looks like. Perplexity answers in a few paragraphs and cites its sources inline. If your page is one of those sources, you are in the conversation. If it is not, the buyer may never see your name.

This guide is the short version of how we approach Perplexity for clients. Each section starts with the answer, then the detail.

Chapter 01/ 081 min

What is Perplexity SEO?

The short answer

Perplexity SEO is the work of getting your pages cited in Perplexity's answers. Perplexity runs a live search for each question, reads a small set of pages and writes an answer with numbered citations. The goal is not a rank position. It is to be one of the sources it quotes.

Google gives a buyer a list of links and lets them do the reading. Perplexity does the reading for them. That changes what a good page looks like: it has to be easy to reach, easy to quote and clearly the best answer to one question.

Search engine vs answer engine: what changes
Compared onGoogle searchPerplexity
What the buyer getsA ranked list of linksA written answer with cited sources
Typical questionShort keywordsFull questions and follow-ups
What you winA position on the pageA citation inside the answer
What helps mostLinks, relevance, page experienceClear answers, structure, trusted sources, freshness
How you measure itRank and clicksHow often you are cited, and next to whom
i

TipSEO still matters here.

Perplexity retrieves pages from the open web. Pages that are crawlable, well linked and already strong in search are the ones it is most likely to find. Treat Perplexity work as a layer on top of good SEO, not a replacement for it.

Chapter 02/ 081 min

How does Perplexity choose its sources?

The short answer

For each question, Perplexity searches the web in real time, pulls a small set of pages and writes the answer from them, citing each one. Pages that are reachable, readable and answer the question directly are the ones it can use.

The process behind every answer runs in four steps. None of them is secret, and each one points to something you can control.

  1. It reads the question. Perplexity works out what is being asked, including context from earlier questions in the same thread.
  2. It searches the live web. Its crawler, PerplexityBot, keeps an index, and the search runs at the moment of the question, so new pages can be used quickly.
  3. It picks and reads a few sources. Only a handful of pages make it into an answer. Clear, specific and trustworthy passages win.
  4. It writes the answer and cites. Each claim links to the page it came from. That citation is the visibility you are working for.

Why a short answer beats a long build-up

Perplexity quotes passages, not whole pages. A section that answers its heading in the first two or three sentences gives it something clean to lift. A section that saves the answer for the fourth paragraph gives it nothing.

Chapter 03/ 081 min

Can Perplexity reach and read your pages?

The short answer

Only if your robots.txt lets PerplexityBot in and your key content is in the raw HTML. Perplexity publishes its crawler names. Blocking them, or loading your copy with JavaScript, keeps your pages out of its answers.

Start with robots.txt. Many sites block unknown bots by default, or copied a rule that blocks every AI crawler. Check that PerplexityBot is allowed on the pages you want cited.

Then check what the crawler actually sees. Most AI crawlers read the HTML your server sends and do not run JavaScript. If your pricing, comparison table or FAQ only appears after scripts load, it may be invisible to them.

!

Watch outTest with JavaScript off.

Turn JavaScript off in your browser and reload a key page. Whatever disappears is likely missing for AI crawlers too. Fix it with server-side rendering or static generation for the pages that matter most.

Chapter 04/ 081 min

How do you write pages Perplexity can cite?

The short answer

Lead every section with a direct answer, use question-style headings and back your claims with sources. A clear 40 to 60 word answer under each heading is far easier for Perplexity to quote than a long introduction.

The Perplexity playbook

Do

  • Write for the question a buyer actually asks, in their words
  • Use headings, short paragraphs, lists and tables
  • Build topic clusters: one pillar page linked to focused spoke pages
  • Add FAQ and Article schema that matches the visible page
  • Name a real author with real expertise, and cite primary sources

Don't

  • Stuff keywords or repeat the same phrase in every heading
  • Publish walls of text with the answer buried at the end
  • Spread one topic thin across many near-duplicate pages
  • Publish anonymous pages with no author or sources
  • Ignore crawl access and page health

Label the page with schema

Structured data tells machines what each part of a page is: the article, the author, the questions and answers. It does not rescue content a crawler cannot read, but on a readable page it removes guesswork. Keep it honest: the schema must match what a visitor sees.

Chapter 05/ 081 min

How often should you update pages for Perplexity?

The short answer

Update a page when something real changes: new data, a new feature, a better example. Perplexity searches the live web, so a current, clearly dated page has an edge on questions where recency matters.

  • Show a visible "Updated" date, and keep it in step with dateModified in your Article schema.
  • Change the substance, not just the date. A new date on old content is easy to spot.
  • Update the lastmod date in your sitemap when a page really changes.
  • Refresh comparison and pricing-related pages first. Those go stale fastest.

Chapter 06/ 081 min

Do third-party mentions help you get cited?

The short answer

Yes. For many questions Perplexity cites reviews, forums, videos and industry publications next to brand sites. Being described accurately in those places gives it more sources that name you.

Think of Perplexity visibility in two parts. Owned is your own site: the pages you control. Earned is everything else that mentions you: community threads, reviews, independent comparisons and press. Both feed the same answers.

  • Answer real questions in the communities your buyers use, and say who you are.
  • Publish short, screen-recorded tutorials for the jobs your product does.
  • Pitch independent "best tools for X" articles with facts, not adjectives.
  • Publish original research other people want to cite.
i

NoteNever fake it.

Sock-puppet accounts and undisclosed self-promotion get removed, and they damage trust in the brand they were meant to help. Disclose, add value, and let the answer stand on its own.

Chapter 07/ 081 min

How do you track Perplexity visibility?

The short answer

Ask Perplexity a fixed set of buyer questions every month and record whether you are cited, where, and who is cited instead. Pair that log with referral traffic from perplexity.ai in your analytics.

Rank trackers cannot see citations, so start with a simple log. Pick 20 to 30 questions your buyers ask, run them on the same day each month and keep the results in one sheet.

Questions/ 081 min

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to get cited by Perplexity?

It depends on your starting point. Fixing crawl access and rewriting key pages answer-first can show up quickly, because Perplexity searches the live web. For a full program we plan on an audit in week one and initial results, or leading indicators, within 60 to 90 days.

Is Perplexity SEO different from ChatGPT SEO?

The foundations are the same: crawlable pages, clear answers, trusted sources. The differences are in which crawlers to allow and which third-party sources each engine leans on. Our ChatGPT SEO guide covers the ChatGPT side.

Do I need an llms.txt file for Perplexity?

No. It is optional, and it cannot make unreadable content readable. Allowing PerplexityBot and keeping your content in the raw HTML matter far more. If you want to try one, it takes a few minutes with our llms.txt generator.

Should I block Perplexity's crawler?

Only if you do not want to appear in Perplexity at all. Perplexity says PerplexityBot is used to surface and link websites in its search results, not to train AI models. Blocking it removes you from the answers your buyers read.

Can we do this in-house?

Yes, for a small set of pages. The checklist in this guide is enough to start. It gets harder at scale: many pages, schema, refresh cycles and earned mentions across several engines. That is where a GEO program helps.
