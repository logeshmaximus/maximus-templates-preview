Methodology

How we built this list

Every agency went through the same four steps. The weights for each criterion are in the box at the top of the page.

  1. 01

    Build the long list

    We started from agencies that publicly offer AEO or GEO, plus names buyers mention in calls. Agencies with no published AI search work were dropped.

  2. 02

    Test live prompts

    We asked ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Mode the questions each agency's clients care about, and checked who actually gets cited.

  3. 03

    Check the proof

    Case studies had to name the client, the engine and the result. Where a claim could not be checked, we asked for a reference.

  4. 04

    Score and review

    Each agency was scored on the five weighted criteria. A second reviewer checked every score before the list went live.

DisclosureMaximusLabs is a GEO and AEO agency, and we wrote this list. No agency paid to be included or to change its position. On a live page, this box also says how we treat our own listing.

Frequently asked questions

What does an AEO agency do?

An AEO agency gets your brand cited and recommended in AI answers: ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude and Google AI Overviews. The work covers answer-ready content, technical fixes such as schema and crawler access, and earning mentions on the sources AI engines trust.

How is AEO different from SEO?

SEO aims for a high position in a list of links. AEO aims to be part of the answer itself. The two share foundations, like crawlable pages and authority, but AEO is measured in citations and mentions, not rankings. Read AEO vs SEO.

How much does an AEO agency cost?

Most agencies charge a monthly retainer, and the price depends on scope: how many engines, how much content and whether off-site work is included. The prices on this page are samples. For what MaximusLabs charges, see our pricing.

How long until we see results?

Expect an audit in the first week, then initial results, or leading indicators, within 60 to 90 days. Citations build up as AI engines recrawl your pages and the sources they trust.

What should I ask an agency before I hire it?

Ask which engines they track, how they report citations, and how they connect AI visibility to pipeline. Ask for a case study that names the client and the result. If the answer is only keyword rankings, keep looking.

Why does this page use sample brands?

It is the template for every "best X" page on the site. The real brands each page may name are decided before it goes live, under our content rules.
