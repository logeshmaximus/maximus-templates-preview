It started as a small experiment. A warm intro, two calls, a lot of brainstorming, and one simple deal: three months to prove this could work. What follows is exactly what happened next, in Ishan's words and in Oliv AI's own dashboards.

The 30-second version

Start as an experiment. Become a pipeline channel.

Oliv AI gave us three months to prove that generative engine optimization (GEO) could move real revenue, not vanity traffic. That was the 90-day goal. The first inbound qualified leads landed within weeks, and today the program has produced $47K+ in pipeline from 27 leads, with a 26% close rate.

The challenge

Win high-intent buyers against billion-dollar incumbents who invented sales-tech SEO, with a fraction of their resources and zero existing authority.

What we did

Bottom-of-funnel content, engineered so AI engines retrieve and cite it, written from the founder's interviews. End to end, strategy to maintenance.

The outcome

$47K+

pipeline from 27 leads, with a 26% close rate. And oliv.ai became the #2 most-cited domain in its category.

The challenge

Going up against the companies that invented SEO

How the decision actually unfolded, one step at a time.

  1. 1

    The situation

    The hardest category online

    Billion-dollar incumbents wrote the sales-tech SEO playbook. A decade head start, and a well-funded page on every keyword.

  2. 2

    The frustration

    A great product nobody could find

    Oliv AI had a sharp point of view and almost no organic footprint. Most founders see that wall and quietly give up on search.

  3. 3

    The risk

    Invisible for 70% of the journey

    G2 and 6sense: 70% of the buyer journey is over before they reach your site. Do nothing, and it all happens without you.

  4. 4

    The decision

    Run the experiment anyway

    So Oliv AI gave us three months: a 90-day goal to prove GEO moves real revenue, not vanity traffic, or walk away.

Not found, not shortlisted.

Buyers ask AI comparison questions now: alternatives to the market leader, or an all-in-one revenue tool to consolidate their stack. AI rewrites each question into several searches and pulls the pages that match. That is rung 2, Retrieve, of the MaximusLabs Answer Ladder: "AI doesn't find us." A brand without those pages is not in the answer.

The proof

Pipeline first. Then the signals that led to it.

We report revenue first. Oliv AI's program produced $47K+ in pipeline from 27 leads, with a 26% close rate. The curve below is Oliv AI's own Google Search Console: a leading indicator that shows the climb, not the result.

The approach

Articles are not written for humans anymore. They are data sources for AI.

Classic SEO agencies still write for clicks and impressions; AI engines read pages as passages. The moment we built for that, everything about how we made content changed.

An article today is rarely read end to end by a human. It is read by a machine, parsed, and turned into an answer. So the job is not the prettiest blog. It is to be the most trustworthy, most exhaustive source on a topic, the one the model cannot leave out. We made Oliv AI a source AI cites and a brand it recommends.

One article, engineered for every engine

A buyer's research is scattered across a dozen surfaces. We do not pick one. Every Oliv AI article is built to be retrieved and cited across all of them at the same time.

  • Google Search
  • Google AI Mode
  • Google AI Overviews
  • Perplexity
  • Claude

The strategy

Revenue over vanity. Bottom of the funnel, first.

From day one we took a position: we would never optimize for clicks that do not convert. No top-of-funnel fluff. No impression-chasing. We went straight for the buyers closest to a purchase decision, the people already comparing tools.

If a person is searching for a competitor, they are a relevant buyer to us. That was the entire logic. So we attacked the bottom-of-funnel competitor keywords first, exhausted them, and only then moved up the funnel. Every piece had to earn a sales-qualified lead, not a vanity metric.

We went straight at the incumbents

A funnel, not a spray. We started at the sharpest, highest-intent keywords, then widened outward, one layer at a time.

  1. 1 · Highest intent

    • [Competitor] alternatives
    • [Competitor] pricing
    • [Competitor] reviews
    • [Competitor] vs Oliv AI

    Switchers in active evaluation. The closest buyers to a decision, so we started here.

  2. 2 · Category

    • Revenue intelligence
    • Revenue orchestration
    • Conversation intelligence
    • Best revenue AI tools

    Owned only after the competitor terms were exhausted.

  3. 3 · Adjacent

    • Sales tech stack
    • Forecasting accuracy
    • Deal inspection
    • Pipeline hygiene

    The broad layer, built last, on a foundation that already ranks.

How Oliv AI climbed the Answer Ladder

Every brand is stuck on a different rung, and each rung needs different work. Oliv AI started at rung 2, then climbed. Deep before broad: one topic first, to build authority on one thing, then wide.

  1. Rung 2 · Retrieve

    Get found for the buying questions

    "AI doesn't find us." Oliv AI had almost no organic footprint, so we built the bottom-of-funnel pages that match the searches AI runs: deep on revenue intelligence first, then across the category.

  2. Rung 3 · Rerank

    Stay in the answer

    "AI finds us, then drops us." Every page opens with an answer-first passage and carries the founder's point of view and data, so engines keep it when they cut pages down to passages.

  3. Rung 4 · Reinforce

    Earn the corroboration

    "AI uses our content but names our competitors." The work now is the third-party mentions AI checks before it names a brand, so Oliv AI moves from cited to recommended.

The execution

End to end. Strategy to maintenance, one team owns all of it.

No handoffs, no "here is your content, good luck." GEO strategists plan and decide; GEO engineers build. One team runs the whole channel, from the first keyword decision to the refresh that keeps a ranking alive.

  1. 1

    Interview

    GEO strategists interview Ishan and study sales calls for the questions buyers actually ask.

  2. 2

    Plan

    They pick the rung to work on and the few pages that bring most of the pipeline.

  3. 3

    Write

    In the founder's voice, from the interviews, so it sounds like Ishan wrote it.

  4. 4

    Engineer

    GEO engineers structure every page so engines can retrieve and cite it: schema, clean HTML, AI crawlers unblocked.

  5. 5

    Track

    Rankings and AI citations as leading indicators; leads and pipeline as the result.

  6. 6

    Refresh

    Pages refreshed before they go stale, and new clusters added as the category moves.

How every single article gets made

Our own technology holds the standard on every page: a brand knowledge base, quality checks against the client's rules, and reviews by senior strategists.

Context

A brand knowledge base

  • Ishan's point of view and product facts
  • Weekly competitor and market tracking
  • Every writer on the same positioning

Checks

Quality checks against Oliv AI's rules

  • Answer-first passages, dense with data
  • Automated schema and ranking checks
  • Refreshes before pages go stale

Review

Senior strategists sign off

  • Every page read through the ICP's eyes
  • Rewritten until it meets the bar
  • Strategy reviewed every two weeks
