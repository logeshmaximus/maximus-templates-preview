Timeline · 2011 to 2025

Google algorithm updates, from Panda to AI Mode

Fifteen years of Google updates in one timeline. Each one pushed search the same way: from matching keywords to understanding questions, and now to answering them.

Google changes its ranking systems thousands of times a year. A few changes were big enough to get a name. This timeline covers the ones that changed how SEO is done, grouped by era, with what each one meant in practice.

Era 01/ 051 min

How did Google fight low-quality content and link spam?

The short answer

With two updates in 2011 and 2012. Panda demoted thin, low-value pages. Penguin demoted sites that built links to game rankings. Together they ended the era of ranking through volume alone.

  1. Feb 2011Quality

    Panda

    Google rolled out a change aimed at reducing rankings for low-quality sites and lifting high-quality ones. It hit content farms hardest.

    Key impact
    Sites full of thin or copied pages lost visibility across the board.
    Effect on SEO
    Content quality became a site-wide signal, not a page-level detail.
    Google blog
  2. Apr 2012Spam

    Penguin

    An update targeting webspam, especially manipulative link schemes and keyword stuffing.

    Key impact
    Paid and artificial links turned from an asset into a risk.
    Effect on SEO
    Link building shifted to earning links from real, relevant sites.
    Google Search Central blog
Era 02/ 051 min

When did Google start understanding questions, not just keywords?

The short answer

From 2013. Hummingbird rebuilt the core algorithm around the meaning of whole queries, and BERT in 2019 let Google read each word in the context of the words around it. Matching exact keywords stopped being the game.

  1. Sep 2013Core

    Hummingbird

    A rewrite of Google's core algorithm, announced at its 15th anniversary, built to understand the meaning of whole queries.

    Key impact
    Conversational and long queries got better answers.
    Effect on SEO
    Topics and intent started to matter more than exact-match keywords.
  2. Oct 2019AI

    BERT

    Google applied BERT language models to ranking and featured snippets, so it could understand the context of words in a search.

    Key impact
    Small words like "for" and "to" started to change results.
    Effect on SEO
    Clear, precise writing that answers the real question gained ground.
    Google blog

Each step made Google better at reading like a person. That same shift is why answer-first pages now get cited by AI engines. Our AEO guide picks up the story.

Era 03/ 051 min

How did page experience and helpful content change rankings?

The short answer

Google added mobile-friendliness in 2015 and page experience, including Core Web Vitals, in 2021. In 2022 the helpful content system started rewarding content written for people, and in March 2024 it became part of the core ranking systems.

  1. Apr 2015Mobile

    Mobile-friendly update

    Mobile-friendliness became a ranking signal for searches on mobile devices.

    Key impact
    Pages that did not work on phones lost mobile rankings.
    Effect on SEO
    Responsive design became the default.
    Google Search Central blog
  2. Jun 2021Experience

    Page experience update

    Google began rolling out page experience signals, including Core Web Vitals, as part of ranking.

    Key impact
    Loading, responsiveness and visual stability got measured in the field.
    Effect on SEO
    Speed work moved from nice-to-have to a tie-breaker.
  3. Aug 2022Quality

    Helpful content update

    A new system aimed at content made mainly to rank in search rather than to help people.

    Key impact
    Sites with lots of unhelpful content saw site-wide drops.
    Effect on SEO
    Experience, expertise and original insight became the brief.
    Google Search Central blog
  4. Mar 2024Core

    March 2024 core update

    A large core update that folded the helpful content system into Google's core ranking systems, alongside new spam policies.

    Key impact
    Helpfulness stopped being a separate check and became part of every ranking.
    Effect on SEO
    Scaled, low-value content became a policy risk, not just a ranking risk.
    Google Search Central blog
Era 04/ 051 min

What changed when Google started answering with AI?

The short answer

In May 2024 Google brought AI Overviews to everyone in the US, putting a written answer with links above the results. In 2025 AI Mode went further: a full conversational search experience. Being cited inside the answer now matters as much as ranking below it.

  1. May 2024AI

    AI Overviews

    Announced at Google I/O: AI-generated overviews with links to sources, rolling out to all users in the US first.

    Key impact
    Many questions now get answered before any blue link.
    Effect on SEO
    Being one of the cited sources became its own goal.
    Google blog
  2. May 2025AI

    AI Mode

    After a Labs test earlier in the year, Google rolled out AI Mode in the US: a conversational search mode that breaks a question into many searches and writes one answer.

    Key impact
    Search started to look like a chat with follow-up questions.
    Effect on SEO
    Pages need to answer the whole cluster of questions around a topic.

Questions/ 051 min

Frequently asked questions

How often does Google update its algorithm?

Constantly. Google makes many small changes every year and announces a few broad core updates. It lists confirmed ranking updates on its Search Status Dashboard.

What should I do after a core update hits my site?

Don't rush fixes. Compare the pages that dropped with the ones that now rank, and ask honestly which is more helpful. Improve the content itself, then give it time: recovery often shows at the next core update.

Do Google updates affect AI answers too?

Yes, for Google's own AI features. AI Overviews and AI Mode draw on pages that are indexed and eligible to show in Search, so the same quality signals apply. Other engines such as ChatGPT and Perplexity run their own systems.
